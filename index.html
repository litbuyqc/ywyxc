Oopbuy shopping guide

Turn a long list
into a good decision.

The most useful Oopbuy spreadsheet finds are the ones you can evaluate. Keep a small set of comparable options and record what is known, what has changed, and what still needs checking.

This directory is a browsing companion. It does not provide an Oopbuy login, account integration, checkout, or live inventory feed.

  1. Choose one category and a clear purpose.

    Start with everyday shoes, a layering piece, or one accessory. A specific use makes it easier to compare similar products instead of collecting unrelated links.

  2. Record the exact version.

    Save the product URL, seller, model, color, size, and date checked. Two listings with similar photographs may describe different materials, construction, or included items.

  3. Check measurements against something you own.

    For an Oopbuy shoe spreadsheet, record the relevant sizing chart and foot-length guidance. For an Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet, compare garment measurements with a well-fitting item laid flat.

  4. Compare the full expected cost.

    Keep item price and currency separate from delivery, service charges, payment fees, and any applicable import costs. Mark unconfirmed amounts as estimates rather than treating them as final quotes.

  5. Reopen the destination before ordering.

    Check the selected variant, availability, seller terms, and return conditions again. A saved spreadsheet entry can remain visible after the underlying listing changes.

Suggested columns for your own product shortlist
Field What to record Why it matters
Product identity Source URL, seller name, exact model, color, and variant. Keeps visually similar listings from being treated as identical.
Fit Size system, garment measurements, or relevant shoe-size guidance. Makes the comparison about dimensions rather than labels alone.
Material & finish Stated composition, lining, hardware, and care instructions. Helps identify differences that a thumbnail may hide.
Visual evidence Front, back, side, detail photos, and any verified inspection notes. Shows what has actually been documented and what remains uncertain.
Cost & currency Item price, estimated charges, currency, and estimate date. Prevents a low item price from being confused with the delivered total.
Availability & terms Date checked, stock status, dispatch information, and return conditions. Highlights information that can change after a link is saved.

Common questions about spreadsheet lists, category browsing, and product checks.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping finds associated with Oopbuy. Depending on its creator, it may include categories, photos, prices, seller links, and notes. Entries should be checked against the current destination listing.

Is this the official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent CAFANS category directory and educational guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, not to an Oopbuy account, checkout, or official service.

Can I download a spreadsheet from this page?

No spreadsheet download is provided here. This page offers a browser-based category directory and a comparison method you can use in your own spreadsheet.

How should I compare shoes and clothing?

Match the exact model, color, size, and material first. For shoes, compare the seller's size chart with a comfortable pair you own. For clothing, compare garment measurements with an item that fits. Then check current photos, availability, and the full expected cost.

Does a spreadsheet link prove authenticity or guarantee delivery?

No. A spreadsheet entry or reference image does not verify authenticity, seller reliability, stock, or delivery. Check the current seller information, product evidence, payment terms, and applicable return policy before ordering.

What should I do if a product link stops working?

Record the link as unavailable and return to the relevant category to look for a comparable item. Recheck the replacement's model, measurements, price, and seller details rather than assuming it matches the original.
